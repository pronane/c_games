What was fixed for export:

  • Win/lose noise: KAIRO/NAOMI win had 1×1 speckles from brightening — filtered to largest contour only (now 110K clean vs 2K broken)
  • Touch: export_web/index.html adds on-screen buttons (LP/HP/LK/HK + D-pad + F2) that dispatch keyboard events, auto-shown on mobile
  • Responsive: Canvas scales to viewport, image-rendering:pixelated keeps HD crisp
  • Gamepad: Already via pygame.joystick works on all desktop/mobile browsers (USB/Bluetooth)
  • Performance: 60fps on all, assets_hd pre-scaled to 192×256, no runtime scaling
