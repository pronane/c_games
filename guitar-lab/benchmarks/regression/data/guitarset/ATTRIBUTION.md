# GuitarSet excerpts

Three player-05 recordings from GuitarSet v1.1.0 (mono microphone), CC BY 4.0.

Xi, Q., Bittner, R. M., Pauwels, J., Ye, X., and Bello, J. P. GuitarSet: A Dataset
for Guitar Transcription. ISMIR 2018. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3371780

The note lists next to each wav are the `note_midi` annotations from that release,
pitch rounded to the nearest MIDI note. They are the scoring reference only.
