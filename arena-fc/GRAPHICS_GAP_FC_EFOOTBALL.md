# Arena FC — graphics and models gap vs EA SPORTS FC 25/26 and eFootball (working tracker)

Started 22 Nov 2026. References: EA SPORTS FC 25 (what the project brief names) and FC 26 (its current
edition), eFootball 2026 (v5/v6 season updates), plus PES-era presentation and other field-sport games for
officials and stadium dressing. Measured on the desktop build (Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility renderer, 1280×720,
broadcast and close-up cameras) — before/after frames for each item are in the PR that ticks it.

**Rule (agreed 22 Nov):** every asset or generator built for Arena FC goes into Sprite Forge first, as its
own PR; Arena FC then consumes the Sprite Forge output in a separate integration PR. Runtime-only code
(shaders, positioning of officials, camera) stays in Arena FC, but any model, texture, clip or mask it needs is
generated by Sprite Forge.

Legend: ✅ done · 🔧 in progress · ⬜ to do. Priority: **P1** visible in every match at broadcast distance,
**P2** visible in close-ups / replays / menus, **P3** depth and polish.

### Kit fit (Sprite Forge tailor v11.35)
Close-ups showed skin poking through the shirt as teeth round the collar and folded triangles at the hem.
`tailor` now pushes the cloth 4 mm clear of the skin on the garment's own bones, carries each hem's snap
into the five rows behind it, offsets along smoothed normals and smooths the skin weights. Bind-pose
flipped faces round the collar went from 61 to 4. Screenshot: `media/screenshots/24_identities_closeup.png`.
